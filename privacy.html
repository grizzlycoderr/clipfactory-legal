Privacy Policy

ClipFactory · last updated 9 November 2026

ClipFactory is a desktop application for a single creator's own use. It turns the user's own long videos into short vertical videos and can publish them to the user's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

What data the app processes

  • Videos, subtitles and edits the user creates in the app.
  • After the user signs in with a platform (for example TikTok Login Kit): the access token, the account's display name and avatar, and statistics (such as view counts) of videos the app published.

Where the data is stored

All data, including access tokens, is stored only on the user's own computer. ClipFactory has no server. We do not collect, sell or share any data with third parties, and the app contains no advertising or tracking.

What is sent to platforms

Only when the user clicks Publish does the app send the chosen video, caption and privacy setting to the platform the user selected, using that platform's official API. The app does not read other users' data, messages or comments.

Revoking access and deleting data

The user can disconnect any account in the app (Settings › Accounts), which deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored data.

Platform policies

Use of each platform also falls under its own terms and privacy policy, for example the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
